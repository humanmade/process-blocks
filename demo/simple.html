A clean, bright cup in about four minutes. Work through each step, and mark it complete when you're done; your progress is saved in this browser.

Bring about 500ml of water to the boil, then let it rest for 30 seconds. You're aiming for 92–96°C.

Weigh out 15g of beans and grind them medium-fine, about the texture of table salt.

Place the paper filter in the dripper and pour hot water through it. This removes any papery taste and warms the dripper. Discard the rinse water.

Add the grounds, level the bed, and pour 30g of water to saturate them. Wait 30–45 seconds while the coffee releases gas.

  • Pour slowly in circles to 150g.
  • Let it draw down slightly, then pour to 250g.

Once the water has drained through (around 3:30 total), remove the dripper, give the coffee a swirl, and enjoy.
